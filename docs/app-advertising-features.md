# Wishlane Advertising Feature Brief

## Core Positioning

Wishlane is a wishlist and gift coordination app for people who want better gift ideas and smoother gift planning.

Primary promise:

- Save gift ideas in one place.
- Share wishlists with the right people.
- Let friends reserve gifts so nobody buys the same thing twice.
- Discover what friends actually want.
- Plan Secret Santa exchanges without spreadsheets or chat chaos.

Short positioning options:

- Wishlists that make gift giving easier.
- The simpler way to share and plan gifts.
- Save what you want. Share it when it matters.
- Gift ideas, wishlists, reservations, and Secret Santa in one place.
- Stop guessing. Start gifting from real wishlists.

## Audience Segments

### Gift receivers

These users create wishlists for themselves.

What they care about:

- Getting gifts they actually want.
- Avoiding awkward conversations about what to buy.
- Keeping gift ideas organized throughout the year.
- Sharing different lists with different people.
- Adding products quickly when they find something online.

Ad angles:

- "Make it easy for people to buy you something you actually want."
- "Keep every gift idea in one place, then share the right list when someone asks."
- "Create wishlists for birthdays, holidays, weddings, housewarming, baby showers, or everyday ideas."

### Gift buyers

These users browse other people's wishlists and reserve gifts.

What they care about:

- Knowing what someone wants.
- Avoiding duplicate gifts.
- Staying within budget.
- Seeing prices, stores, images, and links.
- Marking gifts as reserved or purchased.

Ad angles:

- "Know what to buy before the last-minute panic."
- "Reserve gifts so friends and family do not buy the same thing."
- "See gift ideas with links, prices, and status in one place."

### Families and friend groups

These users coordinate gifts across a social circle.

What they care about:

- Sharing lists privately.
- Managing access for close friends or family.
- Organizing people into groups.
- Seeing upcoming events.
- Coordinating birthdays, holidays, and special occasions.

Ad angles:

- "One place for the whole family to share wishlists."
- "Private lists for close friends, family, or selected groups."
- "Plan gifts together without messy group chats."

### Secret Santa organizers

These users create and manage gift exchanges.

What they care about:

- Inviting participants.
- Setting a budget and date.
- Assigning gift receivers fairly.
- Avoiding manual draw logistics.
- Helping participants find gift ideas.

Ad angles:

- "Run Secret Santa without slips of paper or spreadsheets."
- "Invite friends, set a budget, launch the draw, and see who you are buying for."
- "Use wishlists to make Secret Santa gifts easier to choose."

### Online shoppers

These users find items across shopping sites.

What they care about:

- Saving products quickly.
- Avoiding copy-paste work.
- Keeping product image, price, and link attached.
- Saving from major shopping sites.

Ad angles:

- "Found the perfect gift idea? Save it to Wishlane in one click."
- "Add products from Amazon, eBay, Etsy, Walmart, AliExpress, and more."
- "The browser extension captures product details so your wishlist stays complete."

### Power users and Pro prospects

These users may hit Free plan limits or want advanced coordination.

What they care about:

- More wishlists.
- More items per wishlist.
- Price and sale awareness.
- Collaboration.
- Advanced sharing.
- Priority support.

Ad angles:

- "Upgrade when your gift planning outgrows the basics."
- "Unlimited wishlists and unlimited items for people who plan gifts year-round."
- "Unlock sale alerts, price tracking, collaborative wishlists, and advanced sharing."

## Feature-To-Benefit Map

### Wishlists

Feature:

- Users create named wishlists with descriptions, images, event dates, visibility, and visual accents.

Customer benefit:

- Users can organize gift ideas by occasion, person, or theme instead of keeping links in notes, messages, or browser tabs.

Ad message:

- "Create a wishlist for every occasion."
- "Keep birthday, holiday, wedding, and personal gift ideas organized."

### Wishlist items

Feature:

- Items can include name, description, price, discount price, currency, store link, image, priority, and extra links.

Customer benefit:

- Gift buyers get the context they need to choose confidently.

Ad message:

- "Add the details that make a gift easy to buy: links, prices, images, and notes."
- "Give friends better clues than a vague text message."

### Gift reservations

Feature:

- Items can be available, reserved, or purchased.

Customer benefit:

- Friends and family can coordinate without spoiling the surprise or duplicating gifts.

Ad message:

- "Reserve gifts so nobody buys the same thing twice."
- "Coordinate gifts without ruining the surprise."

### Sharing

Feature:

- Users can generate share links and let others view shared wishlists.

Customer benefit:

- Users can send a wishlist when someone asks what they want, and recipients can act from the link.

Ad message:

- "Share your wishlist with one link."
- "Send one clean wishlist instead of a pile of product links."

### Privacy and access control

Feature:

- Wishlists can be public, friends-only, selected-friends, or private. Owners can grant access to friends and groups.

Customer benefit:

- Users control who sees each list.

Ad message:

- "Keep some lists private and share others with exactly the right people."
- "Share with friends, family, selected groups, or nobody until you are ready."

### Friends

Feature:

- Users can search for friends, send requests, accept requests, and view connected friends.

Customer benefit:

- Gift coordination becomes a shared network instead of a one-off link exchange.

Ad message:

- "Connect with friends and make gift planning easier every year."
- "See the wishlists your friends choose to share."

### Friend groups

Feature:

- Users can create groups with names, colors, icons, descriptions, and members.

Customer benefit:

- Sharing is easier for families, friend circles, coworkers, and event groups.

Ad message:

- "Share a list with your whole family in one step."
- "Organize gift planning by group, not by endless individual invites."

### Discover

Feature:

- Users can browse visible wishlists and items from friends, including available, reserved, and purchased tabs.

Customer benefit:

- Gift buyers can find ideas when they do not know what to buy.

Ad message:

- "Browse gift ideas your friends actually want."
- "Find available gifts before someone else reserves them."

### Upcoming events

Feature:

- Discover can show upcoming friend wishlist events and calendar-style event discovery.

Customer benefit:

- Users can prepare for birthdays and special occasions earlier.

Ad message:

- "Stay ahead of birthdays and big moments."
- "Never let an important gift sneak up on you."

### Secret Santa

Feature:

- Users can create events, invite participants, set a budget and date, launch assignments, and show each participant their receiver.

Customer benefit:

- Organizers can run gift exchanges with less manual work.

Ad message:

- "Set up Secret Santa in minutes."
- "Invite, draw names, and help everyone find a better gift."

### Secret Santa gift suggestions

Feature:

- Participants can see visible wishlist suggestions for their assigned receiver.

Customer benefit:

- Secret Santa buyers get real ideas instead of guessing.

Ad message:

- "Secret Santa is easier when everyone has a wishlist."
- "Get assigned a person and see gift ideas they already picked."

### Notifications

Feature:

- Notifications cover friend updates, item reservations or purchases, and Secret Santa invites.

Customer benefit:

- Users do not miss coordination moments.

Ad message:

- "Stay updated when gifts are reserved, invites arrive, or plans change."
- "Gift planning moves faster when everyone knows what happened."

### Settings and personalization

Feature:

- Users can manage profile, account, notifications, appearance, theme, and currency.

Customer benefit:

- The app feels personal and practical across different users, regions, and preferences.

Ad message:

- "Make Wishlane fit the way you plan gifts."
- "Choose your profile, notifications, theme, and default currency."

### Smart link scraping

Feature:

- Wishlane can extract product details from a link, including product name, price, image, URL, and discount information where available.

Customer benefit:

- Users spend less time manually filling out wishlist items.

Ad message:

- "Paste a product link and let Wishlane fill in the details."
- "Less copy-paste. More gift ideas saved."

### Chrome extension

Feature:

- Users can save products from online stores directly into Wishlane from the browser.

Customer benefit:

- Gift ideas can be captured at the moment of discovery.

Ad message:

- "Save products to your wishlist while you shop."
- "One click turns a product page into a wishlist item."

### Ideas and feedback

Feature:

- Users can submit feature ideas, vote, and track idea status.

Customer benefit:

- Users feel involved in the product direction.

Ad message:

- "Help shape the future of Wishlane."
- "Submit ideas and vote on what should come next."

### Free plan

Feature:

- Free users get up to 5 wishlists, up to 20 items per wishlist, smart link scraping, friends, reservations, real-time notifications, Discover, and themes.

Customer benefit:

- Users can start planning gifts without paying.

Ad message:

- "Start free with the essentials for better gift planning."
- "Create wishlists, share gifts, and coordinate with friends for free."

### Pro plan

Feature:

- Pro users get unlimited wishlists, unlimited items, sale alerts, price tracking and history, collaborative wishlists, advanced sharing, and priority support.

Customer benefit:

- Users who plan lots of gifts can remove limits and unlock advanced coordination tools.

Ad message:

- "Go Pro for unlimited wishlists and deeper gift planning tools."
- "Built for people who plan gifts all year."

## Claims To Use

Use these claims when they match the product surface being advertised:

- Save gift ideas in one place.
- Share wishlists with friends and family.
- Reserve gifts to avoid duplicates.
- Add links, prices, images, discounts, priorities, and notes.
- Create private, public, friends-only, or selected-friends wishlists.
- Organize friends into groups.
- Browse visible wishlists from friends.
- Create and launch Secret Santa events.
- Save products from shopping sites with the Chrome extension.
- Start free with core wishlist and sharing tools.
- Upgrade for unlimited wishlists and advanced planning features.

## Claims To Avoid Or Qualify

Avoid overpromising beyond implemented behavior:

- Do not claim the app guarantees the lowest price.
- Do not claim every shopping site extraction is perfect.
- Do not claim the app buys gifts automatically.
- Do not claim reservations are anonymous unless the product flow explicitly supports that.
- Do not claim Pro-only features are available to Free users.
- Do not claim advanced sharing formats such as QR or PDF in a specific ad unless that surface is implemented and enabled.
- Do not claim price tracking or sale alerts in campaigns for users or environments where those features are unavailable.
- Do not imply private lists are visible to friends.

## AI Prompting Notes

When generating ads from this document:

- Choose one audience segment and one pain point per ad.
- Lead with the customer problem, not the feature name.
- Turn features into concrete outcomes.
- Keep copy warm, useful, and direct.
- Avoid making the product sound like a generic shopping app; focus on gift planning and coordination.
- Mention privacy or access control when the campaign targets families, friends, or sensitive wishlists.
- Mention reservations when the campaign targets gift buyers or groups.
- Mention smart link scraping and the Chrome extension when the campaign targets online shoppers.
- Mention Secret Santa only for exchange-focused or seasonal campaigns.
- Mention Pro only when the user is active, near limits, or already understands the free product.
