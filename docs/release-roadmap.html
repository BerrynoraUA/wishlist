📅 Suggested sequencing

Sprint A — Safety net

  • CI pipeline · Sentry on all surfaces · security/RLS review · lock down admin + cron · rate limiting.

Sprint B — Money & stores

  • Verify RevenueCat live on all 3 surfaces · EAS submit profiles · store records + assets · first TestFlight/internal builds.

Sprint C — Launch readiness

  • Domain/env finalize · data deletion + consent · onboarding finish · extension submission · QA + a11y pass.

Sprint D — Ship & iterate

  • Public launch · monitor dashboards · repo hygiene · growth instrumentation · fast-follow polish.
