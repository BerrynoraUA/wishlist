## Name

Wishlane — Add to Wishlist

## Short Description (132 chars max)

Save any product from the web to your Wishlane wishlists with one click. Works on Amazon, eBay, Etsy, Walmart, AliExpress & more.

## Detailed Description

Wishlane makes it effortless to save products you love while shopping online.

🛒 HOW IT WORKS

1. Browse any online store
2. Click the Wishlane icon in your toolbar
3. The extension automatically detects the product name, price, and image
4. Pick a wishlist and click "Add to Wishlist" — done!

✨ KEY FEATURES
• One-click save — No copy-pasting links or prices.
• Smart product detection — Automatically extracts product details from any page using structured data, meta tags, and smart heuristics.
• Works everywhere — Optimized for Amazon, eBay, Etsy, Walmart, and AliExpress, with fallback support for any online store.
• Wishlist selector — Choose which wishlist to save to right from the popup.
• Discount tracking — Automatically captures sale prices and discount end dates when available.
• Synced with Wishlane — Items appear instantly in the Wishlane web and mobile apps.

🔒 PRIVACY & SECURITY
• Your data is stored securely in your own Wishlane account.
• No browsing history is collected or tracked.
• No ads, no analytics scripts, no third-party tracking.
• Authentication via email/password or Google sign-in.

📋 REQUIREMENTS
• A free Wishlane account (sign up at the Wishlane web app).
