# Privacy Policy — Wishlane Browser Extension

**Last updated:** April 19, 2026

This privacy policy describes how the Wishlane browser extension ("Extension") collects, uses, and protects your information.

## 1. Information We Collect

### Account Information
When you sign in to the Extension, we process your email address and authentication credentials to verify your identity. If you use Google sign-in, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture from Google.

### Product Data
When you click "Add to Wishlist," the Extension reads product information (title, price, image URL, and description) from the currently active web page. This data is only collected when you actively use the Extension — it is **not** collected automatically or in the background.

### Page URL
The URL of the active tab is sent to the Wishlane server for product data extraction only when you open the Extension popup on that page.

## 2. Information We Do NOT Collect

- **Browsing history** — We do not track, record, or transmit your browsing activity.
- **Analytics or telemetry** — The Extension contains no analytics scripts, tracking pixels, or usage telemetry.
- **Personal files** — We do not access files on your device.
- **Other tabs** — We only access the currently active tab, and only when you open the Extension.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

- **Authentication:** To sign you in and keep your session active.
- **Wishlist management:** To save product details to your chosen wishlist.
- **Product extraction:** To read product metadata from the current page so you can review it before saving.

## 4. Data Storage & Security

- Your wishlist data and account information are stored securely on Supabase infrastructure.
- Authentication tokens are stored locally in your browser using `chrome.storage.local` and are never shared with third parties.
- All communication between the Extension and our servers uses HTTPS encryption.

## 5. Third-Party Services

| Service | Purpose | Privacy Policy |
|---------|---------|----------------|
| Supabase | Authentication & data storage | https://supabase.com/privacy |
| Google OAuth | Optional sign-in method | https://policies.google.com/privacy |

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any other third parties.

## 6. Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as you maintain an active Wishlane account. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time through the Wishlane web application.

## 7. Your Rights

You may:
- **Access** your data through the Wishlane web or mobile app.
- **Delete** your account and all stored data at any time.
- **Revoke** Google sign-in permissions via your Google Account settings.

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
